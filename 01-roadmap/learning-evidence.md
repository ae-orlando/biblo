# Learning evidence

Evidence notes live in `01-roadmap/evidence/`. Create one with `:New evidence`
in Neovim or `python3 90-tools/notes.py evidence "Title"`; the factory renders
`99-templates/evidence.md` into `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.

Do not infer mastery or completion from the rebuild. Start with the baseline
diagnostic and add rows below only after doing the work.

| Date | Lab / project | Artifact path | Checks and manual evidence | Assistance / gap | Next review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Each evidence note records the claim, artifact, command and actual result, and
the assistance used. A terminal log alone is not enough: add a one-sentence
explanation of what the result establishes and what it does not establish. Use
[[study-and-review]] for the rubric. Keep sensitive information out of logs.

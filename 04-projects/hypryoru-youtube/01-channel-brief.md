# 01 Channel Brief

## What it is
Screen-only Linux dev lifestyle channel. No facecam. Voiceover over real work: the rice, the tools, the projects, the maintenance.

## Niche
Linux dev lifestyle. Arch + Hyprland as a daily driver — ricing, dev tooling, real full-stack builds, and keeping it all alive. Everything from a repo, reproducible at a pinned commit.

## Why this wins
1. Lua modules, not flat conf. `~/Dev/dotfiles/hyprland/hyprland.lua` + `hyprland/modules/` (9 modules). Most tutorials still teach `hyprland.conf`.
2. Matugen pipeline. One wallpaper regenerates 12 apps. Visual payoff on camera.
3. Reproducer. `~/Dev/dotfiles/install.sh` with `core` vs `full` profiles. Viewer can replicate exactly.
4. YoruNeovim. A real, installable Neovim distro (LazyVim-based) — not a dotfile screenshot.
5. Real builds. Full-stack projects shipped in public (`bachiru-coffee-house`, `emirates-web`, …), not tutorial clones.
6. Maintenance is infinite. Updates, breakage, fixes — search-heavy and always current.

## Audience
Linux users and developers who want a reproducible daily driver: ricers who copy and break, Neovim/terminal users, and dev-build-in-public watchers. They want diffs that work, not screenshots.

## Pillars
- A — Ricing: HyprYoru modules (general, binds, rules, monitors, Waybar, Rofi, Kitty, SwayNC, matugen)
- B — Dev tooling: YoruNeovim, tmux, zsh, terminal workflow (evergreen search)
- C — Build in public: real full-stack project logs
- D — Linux for devs: Arch setup, VMs, break/fix framed for developers
- E — Arch/Linux maintenance: updates, breakage, backups, system care

Rotation: **A-A-X** until video ~20 — two ricing videos, then one rotating pillar (B → C → D → E). Ricing keeps the algorithm signal while the other pillars phase in. After video 20, rebalance by metrics (CTR + retention per pillar).

No new *format types* until 20 videos — new ideas get absorbed into a pillar, not a new series.

## Constraints (voiceover-only)
- 6-12 min. Retention drops after 12 for screen-only.
- 1/week min, 2/week max. More = burnout, less = momentum loss.
- Record silent first, narrate after. Fewer retakes.
- Captions always on. 30%+ watch muted.

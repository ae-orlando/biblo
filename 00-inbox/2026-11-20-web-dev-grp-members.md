## Project
A single-vendor e-commerce web application for a retail shop specializing in clothing, footwear, and fashion accessories.

The platform will serve as the shop's digital storefront, allowing customers to explore available products, view product descriptions and images, check prices, select sizes and colours, add items to their shopping cart, and place orders online.

The application will include an administrative dashboard that enables shop administrators to manage products, monitor inventory, process customer orders, and maintain the store's online catalogue.

### Goal
The goal is to extend the shop's physical retail operations into the digital space, making its products accessible to customers online and providing a convenient shopping experience.
